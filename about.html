Our Story

We started with a camera, a hard drive and an obsession with detail.

Awesome Media Films began as a small crew shooting whatever we could — product films for local businesses, event coverage, brand videos. What stayed constant was the standard: nothing leaves the studio until it looks like it belongs on a screen bigger than the one it was made for.

Today we're a full-service creative production house covering film, photography, editing, motion, design and branding. Different tools, different teams — but the same obsession with getting it right.

Creative Philosophy

Story first.
Always.

Cameras, lenses and lights are just tools. What actually holds a viewer is a story told with clarity and feeling. So we start there — with the idea, the message and the emotion — and only then decide how to shoot it.
